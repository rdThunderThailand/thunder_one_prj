# Review targets and preview plan

## Goal

Make the Review step explain the real delivery impact and use its wide preview
area more effectively.

## Changes

1. Split Where to Play into Screens, Channels, and Channel Groups tabs.
2. Show Channels selected directly and Channels inherited from selected Groups.
3. Balance Preview and Timeline as two fluid columns and enlarge the preview.
4. Use compact icon controls for the embedded preview player.

## Constraints

- Screens remain disabled.
- Review controls are read-only and do not change the draft targets.
- Reuse the existing Channel membership data and preview player.
